# Audio asset

`ticking-clock.wav` is a loop-adjusted PCM copy of the high-quality MP3 preview of **“Loopable Ticking Clock”** by OwlStorm (Ashe Kirk / Owlish Media). It removes the 220 ms lead-in and pads the loop to exactly 4 seconds (60 BPM, in time with the theme at 120 BPM), without an audible tempo shift. `tension-theme.mp3` is a 128 kbps MP3 conversion of **“Tension Theme”** by Umplix, bundled locally from the source WAV.

- Ticking source: https://freesound.org/people/OwlStorm/sounds/212181/ (CC0 1.0 Universal, as stated on the source page).
- Music page: https://opengameart.org/content/tension-theme (CC0, as stated on the source page).
- CC0 deed: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Both creators indicate attribution is optional. This note is kept for provenance. Both tracks are bundled in the app and play together as looping clue ambience.
